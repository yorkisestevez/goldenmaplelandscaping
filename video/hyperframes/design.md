# Golden Maple — video design spec

Concept: **Drawn, then built.** Every piece starts as a DeckCraft drawing (cream sheet, hairline grid, gold
dimension lines) and resolves into the photographic render. The gold dimension line is the throughline: it draws the
plan, underlines the name, and becomes the CTA rule.

## Palette (brand, strict)
- Sheet `#FAF8F4` · Cream `#EAE4DA` · Ink `#292824` · Black `#23221F` · Muted `#68645C`
- Gold `#C6A47D` (rules, marks, accents on dark) · Gold deep `#78563E` (gold text on light, for contrast)
- Over photographs: ink scrims (`rgba(35,34,31,.0→.72)`), cream type.

## Type (three registers)
- Statements: Cormorant Garamond 600/700, tracking -0.02em. Names, headlines.
- Voice/body: Inter 400/600. Taglines, CTA.
- Data: JetBrains Mono 400/700, uppercase, tracking 0.14em. Labels, specs, sheet metadata.

## Do / Don't
- Do anchor type to edges (top-left names, bottom spec panels); keep 120px top / 260px bottom reel safe zones.
- Do keep photographs full-bleed and moving (slow push, drift); never static.
- Don't use gradient text, neon, or more than one accent hue. No prices on screen.
