# DeckCraft landscape starter assets

All listed models are Poly Haven CC0 assets, downloaded for local self-hosting. They are generic visual proxies, not botanical SKUs or supplier products.

- [tree_small_02](https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_small_02) → deciduous-tree (three local GLB LODs)
- [fir_tree_01](https://polyhaven.com/a/fir_tree_01) → conifer-tree (three local GLB LODs)
- [shrub_02](https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_02) → rounded-shrub and hedge-shrub (three shared local GLB LODs)
- [grass_medium_01](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_medium_01) → grass-clump (three local GLB LODs)
- [namaqualand_boulder_03](https://polyhaven.com/a/namaqualand_boulder_03) → natural-boulder (three local GLB LODs)
- [outdoor_table_chair_set_01](https://polyhaven.com/a/outdoor_table_chair_set_01) → outdoor-chair (three local GLB LODs)
- [outdoor_table_chair_set_01](https://polyhaven.com/a/outdoor_table_chair_set_01) → outdoor-table (three local GLB LODs)

[Poly Haven asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license) permits commercial use and redistribution. Source download URLs, selected nodes, byte-range hashes and derived GLB hashes appear in manifest.json. Original files/ranges remain in work/landscape-originals; they are not shipped.

Foliage derivatives preserve generic source appearance with bounded geometry. The deciduous tree uses source-position cards for its original connected leaf UV islands. The evergreen selects the fuller original Fir Tree 01 variant B, with six photographed twig atlas regions on crossed cards placed at its original needle positions. Its canopy is a source-based visual proxy. Shrub and grass keep their original selected geometry at each texture LOD. These meshes do not establish plant species, nursery size or planting spacing.

Mulch: [Wood Chip Path](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_chip_path), CC0, by Dimitrios Savva. Original diffuse/normal/roughness maps retained; local 512 px derivatives use the documented 2.1 m texture span.
