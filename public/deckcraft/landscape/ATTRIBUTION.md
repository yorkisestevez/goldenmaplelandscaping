# DeckCraft landscape assets

Plant library v2 uses Poly Haven CC0 scans and photographs, downloaded for local self-hosting, plus derived summer foliage built from those photographs. They are visual families for Ontario zone 4–5 plantings, not botanical SKUs or supplier products. A species record names the plant; the mesh does not set a price.

- [tree_small_02](https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_small_02) → deciduous-tree, rounded-shrub and hosta-clump
- [fir_tree_01](https://polyhaven.com/a/fir_tree_01) → conifer-tree, hedge-shrub, evergreen-shrub and pine-tree
- [grass_medium_01](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_medium_01) → grass-clump and reed-grass
- [fern_02](https://polyhaven.com/a/fern_02) → fern-clump
- [periwinkle_plant](https://polyhaven.com/a/periwinkle_plant) → perennial-bloom (alpha blend converted to alpha test)
- [celandine_01](https://polyhaven.com/a/celandine_01) → perennial-gold (alpha blend converted to alpha test)
- [namaqualand_boulder_03](https://polyhaven.com/a/namaqualand_boulder_03) → natural-boulder
- [outdoor_table_chair_set_01](https://polyhaven.com/a/outdoor_table_chair_set_01) → outdoor-chair and outdoor-table

[Poly Haven asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license) permits commercial use and redistribution. Source download URLs, selected nodes, byte-range hashes and derived GLB hashes appear in manifest.json.

The hedge is its own columnar needle-card volume. It no longer reuses the old shrub_02 sprig scan. Distant levels of detail are reduced crossed-card impostors that keep the silhouette. Foliage materials use alpha masking so cards do not sort as transparent glass. The deciduous tree still uses source-position cards for its original connected leaf UV islands. The evergreen selects the fuller original Fir Tree 01 variant B.

Mulch: [Wood Chip Path](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_chip_path), CC0, by Dimitrios Savva. Brown, black and cedar beds share that scan. Black is a dark multiply of the same chips. Local 512 px derivatives use the documented 2.1 m texture span.
