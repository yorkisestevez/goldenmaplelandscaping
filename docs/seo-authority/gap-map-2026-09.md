# Competitor gap map — Barrie hardscape, September 2026

The SEO authority campaign's premise is to out-authority competitors rather than trying to match their review counts. Golden Maple's Google Business Profile has 5.0 stars from 8 reviews. This document is the baseline the campaign is measured against. The owner URL for each keyword lives in [`keyword-map.json`](./keyword-map.json).

**The homepage carries the site.** It earned 4,994 of the 10,536 main-domain impressions and 38 of the 71 clicks in the last 90 days. The service pages rank on page 3 and page 4 with essentially no clicks:
- `/services/interlocking-barrie/` sits at position 28 to 31 with 0 clicks over 1,046 impressions. Search Console splits it across the slash and no-slash forms of the URL.

- The redirect decisions are already drafted in `docs/seo-migration/`, but every row is marked OWNER_CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED.
- **The owner needs to decide:** redirect these pages to the matching `.ca` page, or return a 410 for the out-of-area ones.

## 5. Method and limits

- **Rankings come from Search Console, not SERP scraping.** Google blocks automated result pages, and a bypass would breach its terms. We don't have rank data for competitors. A self-hosted tracker (the `serp-tracker` skill, SerpBear) needs a scraping API, which is a paid decision for the owner.
- **Competitor pages come from public sitemaps and page fetches.** Ideal's and Strong Roots' sitemaps returned 403 errors, so those rows rely on individual page fetches.
- **The scripts that produced these numbers** were one-off pulls: GSC query×page with rowLimit 5000, using the Hermes `_lib/google-sa` service account in read-only scope.
- **Re-measure about two weeks after Sprint 1 is live.** Compare against `keyword-map.json` → `baseline90d`.
