# Hydrangea botany for the procedural models

Research date: 2026-10-20.

Each number is tagged in one of two ways:
- **[S:key]** is sourced; the key points to a URL at the end.
- **[EST]** is an estimate.

All hex colours are estimates, converted from RHS codes or from colour words. Ontario bloom timing is taken from Grand Haven, MI (about 43°N, zone 6a), because no Ontario source gave dates.

## H. paniculata 'Limelight'

**Size**
- Mature: 72–96 × 72–96 in [S:PW-LL]. Purdue gives 72–120 × 60–72 in [S:PUR-LL].
- From a #2/#3 pot: about 20–30 × 16–24 in [EST].
- At year 3: about 54–72 × 48–60 in [EST].

**Habit**
- Upright, rounded, dense, multi-stemmed [S:PAT-LL].
- Panicles stand upright on strong peduncles; young plants bow under heavy heads [S:PW-LL].
- Patent plant: about 14 laterals, 0.18–0.20 in thick and 24–59 in long, with internodes of 1.2–1.4 in [S:PAT-LL].
- Mature plant: 8–15 canes, 0.75–1.25 in thick at the base [EST].
- Stems: reddish-brown streaked grey [S:NCSU-LL]. Hex: young #6B2A3A, mature #7A5A3E [EST].

**Leaf**
- Ovate and acuminate, 3.3–3.9 × 1.6–2.2 in, serrulate [S:PAT-LL].
- Opposite, or in whorls of 3 [S:PUR-LL].
- Petiole 0.6–0.7 in [S:PAT-LL].
- Colour: RHS 146A upper, 146C underside [S:PAT-LL]. Hex #4E6A2C / #8FA35E [EST].
- Thick, rough and matte [S:PAT-LL, S:NCSU-LL].
- Fall leaf colour: red/burgundy [S:NCSU-LL].

**Inflorescence**
- Terminal pyramidal panicle on new wood [S:PAT-LL].
- Size: 6–10 × 4.7–7 in [S:PAT-LL]. Purdue gives 10–12 × 6–7 in [S:PUR-LL].
- Stands upright and tilts 20–45° when stems bow [EST].
- Count: about 14 on the patent plant [S:PAT-LL]; 40–80 on a mature plant [EST].

**Florets**
- About 850–1,200 flowers per panicle [S:PAT-LL].
- Sterile floret: 4 elliptic sepals, each 0.4–0.8 × 0.3–0.5 in [S:PAT-LL].
- Floret diameter 1.1–1.85 in [S:PAT-LL, interpreted].
- Fertile florets are tiny, about 0.12 in.

**Colour by month**

| Month | Colour | Hex [EST] | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Late July | Lime | #A9C25B → #BFD27E | [S:PAT-LL] |
| August | Creamy white | #ECEFD6 | [S:CBG-LL] |
| September | Pink | #E6B7BF | [S:PUR-LL] |
| October | Rose to burgundy | #C9798C → #8A3A4C | [S:PW-LL] |
| November to March | Tan heads, persistent | #BFA27E | [S:CBG-LL] |

## H. arborescens 'Annabelle'

**Size**
- Mature: 36–60 × 48–72 in [S:PUR-AN].
- From a #2/#3 pot: about 16–24 × 16–24 in [EST].
- At year 3: about 36–48 × 48 in [EST].

**Habit**
- Rounded, clumping, suckering [S:NCSU-AR].
- **Flops** under heavy heads, and more so after a hard cut [S:TSO-AR].
- Canes: 15–40, 0.25–0.4 in thick, rarely branched, arching 30–60° [EST].
- Stems: grey-brown [S:NCSU-AR]. Hex #8A9A5B new, #8B7A66 old [EST].

**Leaf**
- Ovate to elliptic, acuminate, strongly serrate [S:OSU-AN].
- 3–8 × 2–6 in; use 5 × 3.5 [S:PUR-AN].
- Opposite. Petiole about 1.5 in [EST].
- Hex #3F5E2E upper, #A3B38A underside [EST].
- Matte.

**Inflorescence**
- Globe mophead on new wood, up to 12 in across [S:PUR-AN, S:OSU-AN]; RHS says up to 10 in [S:RHS-AN].
- Depth about 0.75 × width [EST].
- 20–50 heads on a mature plant [EST].

**Florets**
- Almost all sterile [S:PUR-AN].
- 3–5 sepals, each 0.16–0.6 in [S:TSO-AR].
- Floret about 0.6–0.9 in [EST].

**Colour by month**

| Month | Colour | Hex [EST] | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Late June | Apple green | #CBD99E | [S:OSU-AN] |
| July | White | #F2F2E6 | [S:RHS-AN] |
| August | Pale green | #CAD5A4 | [S:CLEM] |
| September | Green-ecru | #B3BB8E | |
| October | Tan | #C1B48F | [S:CLEM] |

Cut to the ground in March [S:UMN, S:TMG].

## H. macrophylla Endless Summer 'Bailmer'

**Size**
- Mature: 36–60 × 36–60 in [S:ES-OR].
- From a #2/#3 pot: about 14–20 × 16–22 in [EST].
- At year 3: about 30–40 × 36–42 in [EST].

**Habit**
- Compact mound [S:PAT-ES].
- 10–25 canes up to 0.2 in thick [S:PAT-ES, EST].
- Stems: new wood #7E9A3C, old wood #9C8A72 [EST].

**Leaf**
- Broadly elliptic, serrate, opposite [S:PAT-ES].
- Use 5 × 3.5 in [EST]; the species range is 4–8 × 3–6 [S:NCSU-HM].
- Petiole 0.4–1.2 in [S:PAT-ES].
- Hex #3B5A2C [EST].
- **Glossy**: roughness about 0.35 [S:NCSU-HM, EST].

**Inflorescence**
- Globe mophead, 3–6 in across [S:PAT-ES]. Wayside gives 8–10 [S:WAY].
- About 100 florets per head [S:PAT-ES].
- 15–35 heads on a mature plant [EST].
- Blooms on old and new wood [S:ES-OR].

**Florets**
- Sterile floret 0.8–1.0 in, with 4 round, overlapping sepals [S:PAT-ES].

**Colour by month**

| Month | Colour | Hex [EST] | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Late June | Green → pink | #D4E0A6 → #EBB0C4 | [S:PAT-ES] |
| July | Pink, RHS 62B–C | #E79CB8 / #F0B6C9 | [S:PAT-ES] |
| August–September | Rebloom pink; old heads antique | #A3386A / #C9D49A | [S:PAT-ES] |
| Winter | Brown | #8E7356 | |

- Blue in acid soil: RHS 100C, #8EA6DA [S:PAT-ES].
- Do not cut back in fall [S:ES-OR].
