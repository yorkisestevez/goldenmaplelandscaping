# DeckCraft competitor comparison — 27 September 2026

Web research on 27 September 2026, with a source for each claim (the source list is at the end). Prices are as published
that day; USD unless noted. Some sites blocked automated reading: techo-bloc.com, lowes.com, homehardware.ca and
Buildertrend's pricing page. Those rows rely on search snippets and are marked unverified. Nothing was signed up for or
submitted.

## Bottom line

1. **Homeowners have no equivalent.** No free tool found combines all of these:
   - a deck and a paver patio in one design;
   - several brands' real stock sizes and laying patterns;
   - an itemized price;
   - the finished design sent to the one contractor who will build it.

   The alternatives each fall short:
   - **Manufacturer deck designers** (Trex, TimberTech, Fiberon, Deckorators, MoistureShield) are single-brand, deck-only
     and feed their own dealer networks.
   - **Retailer designers** (Lowe's, Home Depot, RONA, mostly built on 3D Cloud) price from their own shelves and feed
     their own install services.
   - **Paver makers' tools** are single-brand AR apps, photo visualizers or render services, with quantities but no price.
2. **The pro design tools are more powerful, but the contractor runs them.** VizTerra ($97/mo), PRO Landscape+ ($75/mo),
   Realtime Landscaping ($599) and Chief Architect all do decks and patios. They offer better renders and CAD or
   construction output, but none is a website tool the homeowner uses.
3. **No verified product sells what the white-label plan describes.** That is a homeowner-facing 3D deck designer that a
   small contractor embeds on their site, priced from their own price book, with the design arriving as a lead. The
   nearest are:
   - map-based instant-quote widgets for fences, turf and roofs (FenceFlow, MAPQX, QuoteIQ);
   - AI photo-render widgets with no real stock and no price (OutdoorDesigns AI, Yardcast);
   - Hover's embed, which covers siding, roofing and windows only and needs Enterprise pricing.
4. **Two to watch:**
   - **Outside Logic** (outsidelogic.ca, waitlist): the contractor-side version of DeckCraft's pitch.
   - **DeckPlanr** (waitlist): deck layouts, bill of materials, live pricing and a lead-capture tool, with a large claimed
     user base.

   Neither shows a homeowner embed today.

## Where competitors are stronger

- **Permit-grade output:**
  - Simpson Strong-Tie: permit submittal pages and connector specs.
  - Trex: permit blueprint.
  - MiTek and Decks.com: permit plans, cut lists.

  DeckCraft has construction plans, but no permit package is claimed.
- **Designing on a photo of the customer's own house:**
  - deck tools: Deckorators, MiTek;
  - PRO Landscape+, whose AI Paver Tool (February 2026) lays paver patterns onto a photo;
  - Hover, which builds a 3D model from photos.

  DeckCraft models the house instead.
- **AR on site:** Trex, Fiberon, Unilock UView, Techo-Bloc "View in your space", iScape and Structure Studios YARD.
- **Rendering:** Structure Studios Version 4 (2026): path tracing and a new engine.
- **Reach and human designers:**
  - brand dealer networks; iScape claims nearly 4 million downloads;
  - Yardzen human design ($995–$3,495, with Belgard specs in its YardAI).

## Contractor-side alternatives (what a contractor could buy instead)

**Website quote and visual widgets.** None of these is a 3D deck designer.
- FenceFlow: $129/mo, or $229 white-label; 2D fence map, instant price from the contractor's materials.
- MAPQX: $79–$299/mo, plus $999–$3,000 setup and a 12-month minimum.
- QuoteIQ InstaQuote: on the $299/mo Elite plan.
- OutdoorDesigns AI: $199/mo with a widget; AI renders, no price.
- Yardcast: $249/mo with widget and white-label domain.
- Renoworks Pro (Calgary): embeddable siding, roofing, windows and stone; price on request.
- Configurix and Hive CPQ: pergolas and verandas, aimed at manufacturers.

**Contractor-run design and proposals.**
- VizTerra: $97/mo.
- Bolster (Calgary): $299–$499/mo.
- Houzz Pro: $139–$559/mo.
- Rendered Outdoors: $225 per deck render, $350 permit plans.

**Job management and quoting**, where DeckCraft's leads would land. None has visual design.
- Jobber (Edmonton): $49–$499/mo.
- LMN (Markham): $297–$648/mo.
- SingleOps: $220–$550/mo.
- Service Autopilot: $49–$499/mo.
- Estimate Rocket: $139–$359/mo.
- JobTread: $199/mo + $20 per user.
- Aspire: pricing by sales call.
- Buildertrend: pricing unverified.
- CoConstruct is migrating its customers to Buildertrend.

## Implications for the white-label plan

- **The gap is real, so far.** Nobody verified sells small contractors an embeddable homeowner 3D deck-and-patio designer
  priced from their own price book. The closest white-label products are enterprise-only (3D Cloud, Hover Enterprise) or
  2D and AI-only (FenceFlow, Yardcast).
- **Pricing anchors:**
  - Self-serve website widgets: $129–$299/mo. White-label adds about $100/mo, and some add setup fees ($999–$3,000 at
    MAPQX).
  - Contractor 3D tools: $75–$197/mo.
  - Job-management platforms: $49–$648/mo.
  - Trials run 14–30 days.
- **Positioning.** Lead with "your customer designs it, and the priced design arrives as a lead". Don't lead with "price
  book": Outside Logic's site already says "price it from your own price book". Its pitch is the contractor modelling the
  deck, not the homeowner.
- **Integrations.** Send leads into Jobber and LMN first. Both are Canadian and widely used by landscape contractors,
  and today they only take form requests.
- **Risks.**
  - Outside Logic or DeckPlanr adding a homeowner embed.
  - DeckPlanr's claimed scale ("trusted by 2,400+ deck pros in 111+ countries", unverified).
  - Manufacturers deepening their free tools and networks.
  - Retailer-grade 3D (3D Cloud) moving down-market.

## Watch list

- **Outside Logic** — https://outsidelogic.ca
  - Waitlist only: "We onboard a few builders at a time".
  - Headline: "Design it. Price it. Present it. Build it."
  - Subhead: "Model the deck on your customer's house, price it from your own price book, and hand over the proposal, the
    takeoff and the drawings — all from one model."
  - For contractors. No homeowner embed, pricing, founders or partners shown.
  - Footer: Ontario, Canada. The waitlist asks for province or state and annual deck volume, so it is aiming at the US
    too.
  - Instagram (@outsidelogic, with Simcoe Decks) still claims lead gen and scheduling as well.
  - Public sources don't name its owner. That it is Michael Scott of Simcoe Decks is the owner's knowledge.
- **DeckPlanr** — https://www.deckplanr.com
  - Waitlist, with a demo video.
  - For contractors, dealers, distributors, manufacturers and homeowners.
  - Offers automated layouts, bill of materials, live pricing, framing and post plans, permit-ready documents, and
    "DeckSelector" lead capture.
  - 3D, embedding, white-label and pricing are not shown.
