# Golden Maple Google Ads Plan — Premium Community Targeting

Status: **drafted, not launched**. This file is an approval-gated campaign blueprint. Do not launch spend without Yorkis approval.

## Strategic Shift

The old draft was too Barrie-heavy. Barrie remains useful, but bigger hardscape projects are more likely in estate, waterfront, slope, acreage, and high-equity communities surrounding Barrie.

Primary target communities:

- Midhurst
- Springwater
- Snow Valley
- Anten Mills
- Oro-Medonte
- Horseshoe Valley
- Sugarbush
- Shanty Bay
- Hawkestone
- Innisfil waterfront
- Big Bay Point
- Friday Harbour

Secondary/filtered:

- Alcona
- Lefroy
- Stroud
- Orillia waterfront / Atherley
- Wasaga Beach premium/cottage pockets
- Collingwood / Blue Mountain only for $90K+ work

## Required Google Ads Settings

1. Location option: **Presence only** — people in or regularly in targeted locations.
2. Do not use one broad Simcoe radius.
3. Use exact/phrase match first.
4. Keep Search Partners and Display expansion off until lead quality is proven.
5. Track qualified lead/tier, not just raw form submit.
6. Do not launch or increase spend without approval.

## Launch Gate

Before launching/editing campaigns:

1. Confirm HALT lock is off.
2. Confirm monthly spend cap.
3. Confirm conversion tracking and form submissions are working.
4. Yorkis approves keywords, locations, ads, and budgets.
5. Existing account export is reviewed so we do not delete a working source of leads blindly.
